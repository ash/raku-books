The complete, hands-on **course of the Raku programming language** — five parts of theory and practice, with hundreds of quizzes and exercises and an online playground for running the code. It is hosted at **[course.raku.org](https://course.raku.org)**.

<p><a class="btn-primary" href="https://course.raku.org">Open the full course →</a></p>

### The five parts

1. [Raku essentials](https://course.raku.org/essentials)
2. [Advanced Raku subjects](https://course.raku.org/advanced)
3. [Objects, I/O, and exceptions](https://course.raku.org/oop)
4. [Regexes and grammars](https://course.raku.org/regexes)
5. [Functional, concurrent, reactive, and web programming](https://course.raku.org/paradigms)

Beyond the parts, the course also offers an [addendum of 50 comprehensive exercises](https://course.raku.org/addendum) and a [100-question final test](https://course.raku.org/final-test).
