Great to hear that you reached the end of the book. If you came here earlier than you have read the rest of the book, I would recommend you check the Contents and enjoy the Raku examples.

I hope you loved the way Raku can express your ideas in just a few characters of code. Be careful not to go too far. Avoid creating cryptic programs that are difficult to read.

You can make a significant impact in making Raku a popular programming language. Just start using it bit by bit in your practice and share your experience both online and offline.

If you like to give your feedback, the author would love to receive it via email: andy@shitov.ru.
